Privacy Policy

Effective 30 August 2026. Last updated 30 August 2026.

This policy explains how %VITE_DOMAIN% handles information when you visit the page or pay for a thumbs-up photo. It sits alongside the Terms of Service and follows Art. 13 GDPR.

Who is responsible

The controller is Tobias Stolz / Weird Revenue, c/o Autorenglück #73720, Albert-Einstein-Straße 47, 02977 Hoyerswerda, Germany, an individual based in Germany. Email: ok@ok-rich.com. Full details: legal notice.

The short version

  • No cookies. None. Not even a "necessary" one.
  • No local storage, no fingerprinting, no accounts, no advertising networks.
  • The website itself stores nothing about you. Data appears only when you pay (at Dodo Payments) and when I email you the photo.

Hosting (Hetzner)

The page runs on a server of Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. Server logs hold the technically necessary request data (IP address, time, page, user agent) for 14 days and are then deleted automatically, on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (secure operation). A data processing agreement with Hetzner is in place.

Cookieless analytics (Umami)

I use self-hosted, open-source Umami on the same German server. It sets no cookies, stores no plain IP addresses and builds no personal profiles; it counts page views, clicks on the pay button, referrer, device type and country (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). No consent banner is needed because nothing that is not strictly necessary is stored on or read from your device (§ 25 TDDDG).

The counters on the front page

The page asks my own automation (n8n, on the same Hetzner server) once per visit for two numbers: the total paid so far and the number of visits. To avoid counting the same person repeatedly, the server derives a hash from your IP address plus a secret salt, keeps that hash for twelve hours and then deletes it. The IP address itself is never stored, no profile is built, nothing goes to third parties (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, interest in a count that is correct and not inflatable).

Payment (Dodo Payments)

The pay button leads to a checkout page of Dodo Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, Ireland. There you enter the amount, your email address, your payment details and an optional message. Dodo Payments processes the payment data as its own controller; I receive the amount, your email address, the optional message and a payment reference, never your card details (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, contract). See dodopayments.com/privacy.

Notification and email delivery (n8n, Google Workspace)

After payment, Dodo Payments sends the amount, your email address, the message and a reference to my automation (n8n) on the Hetzner server, which notifies me and sends you a confirmation. I then email you the photo through Google Workspace (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses apply in addition. A data processing agreement is in place through the Google Workspace Cloud Data Processing Addendum (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).

Top contributors board

The display name you enter before payment is shown publicly with the amount, unless you type "anonymous". Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, because the public entry is part of what you buy.

Wall of Thumbs

Your photo only appears in the public gallery if you ask for it in the checkout message field ("wall me"). That is consent under Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and you can withdraw it at any time by email, with effect for the future.

How long anything is kept

  • Your email address and message: deleted 30 days after the photo has been sent, both from the automation and from my mailbox.
  • The visitor hash: twelve hours, then gone.
  • Payment records: kept at Dodo Payments and in my bookkeeping for as long as tax and commercial law require (up to 10 years).
