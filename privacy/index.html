Self-hosted, open-source Umami on the same German server counts page views, clicks on the pay button, referrer, device type and country. 4a. Visitor counter and donation total

The start page calls a public endpoint of my own automation (n8n, Hetzner server in Germany) once per visit and displays two numbers: the sum of payments so far and the number of visits. The server processes your IP address solely to avoid double counting: it derives a hash from the address plus a secret salt, keeps that hash for twelve hours and then deletes it. The address itself is never stored, no profiles are built and nothing is passed to third parties (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Payment (Stripe)

The pay button leads to a checkout page by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Dublin, Ireland, where you enter the amount, your email, payment details and an optional message. Stripe processes payment data as its own controller; I receive amount, email, message and a payment reference, never card details (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). See stripe.com/privacy.

6. Notification and email (n8n, Google Workspace)

After payment, Stripe sends amount, email, message and reference to an automation (n8n) on my Hetzner server, which notifies me and sends you a confirmation. I then email you the photo via Google Workspace (Google Ireland Ltd.). Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses apply (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
